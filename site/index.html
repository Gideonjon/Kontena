Safety

Locked until proven.

Kontena runs on Stellar, a global payment network. The rules for every order live in a smart contract, so nobody, not even us, can move your money outside those rules.

  • Money moves only when a checkpoint is confirmed
  • Automatic refunds if a deadline passes or inspection fails
  • Sign in with your fingerprint or face. No passwords or seed phrases
  • Every payment and sign-off is on a public record you can check
  • Suppliers are identity-checked before they can take an order
