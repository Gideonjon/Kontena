Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information Kontena collects, why, and who we share it with.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone, country and, for suppliers and wallet users, date of birth and address.
  • Orders and messages: items, amounts, delivery deadlines, inspection photos and documents you upload.
  • Payments: payment references and bank details needed to pay you out. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Device data: push notification tokens and basic logs for security and debugging.

Why we use it

To run escrow and group orders, process payments, refunds and payouts, verify users, prevent fraud, send notifications about your orders, and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

  • Paystack, to take payments, issue account numbers and send payouts.
  • FossaPay and our off-ramp partner, for USDC wallets and conversions.
  • Google Firebase, for sign-in and notifications.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our servers and stores uploaded images.
  • The other party to an order, limited to what they need to complete it (for example your name and delivery details).
  • Regulators or law enforcement when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep account and transaction records for as long as your account is open and as long afterwards as financial record-keeping rules require.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information by emailing us. Some records must be kept by law, and we will tell you if that applies.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to production systems is restricted. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your devices updated.

Questions? Write to support@kontena.store.
