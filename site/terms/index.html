Terms of Service

Last updated 25 November 2026

These terms cover your use of Kontena, a trade escrow and group-buying service for importers and suppliers in Nigeria and Ghana. By creating an account you agree to them.

1. What Kontena does

Kontena holds a buyer's payment while a supplier fulfils an order. The deposit (30% of the goods value) is released to the supplier when they accept the order. The balance (70%) is released only after the goods pass inspection. Kontena is not the seller of any goods and does not take ownership of them.

2. Accounts

You must be 18 or older and give accurate details. You are responsible for activity on your account. Suppliers may be asked to verify their business before payouts.

3. Orders, escrow and refunds

  • If a supplier does not accept an order within 3 days, you are refunded in full.
  • If a supplier misses the agreed ship-by date, or the goods fail inspection, the locked 70% balance is refunded to you.
  • The protection fee shown at checkout is charged when the supplier accepts the order. Until then it is refundable.
  • Deposits already released to a supplier are a matter between you and the supplier, although you can raise a dispute with us.

4. Naira wallet

The Naira wallet lets you hold a balance in naira, pay for orders, and receive refunds. You can add money by card, bank transfer or by sending a transfer to the personal account number we issue you through our payment partner. You can withdraw your balance to a bank account in your name. Wallet balances do not earn interest. Limits apply to the balance you can hold and may change as we complete verification and regulatory requirements. A wallet is not a bank account and is not covered by deposit insurance.

5. Fees

A buyer protection fee applies to each order, and a spread applies when earnings are converted and withdrawn to a local bank account. The amounts are shown before you confirm.

6. What you must not do

Do not list or buy illegal, counterfeit or restricted goods, misuse the service for fraud or money laundering, or provide false proof of goods. We may suspend accounts and hold payments while we investigate.

7. Disputes

If you disagree with an inspection result or a refund, contact support within 7 days. We will review the order history and proof and decide in good faith.

8. Liability

We work to keep Kontena available and accurate, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted service. To the extent the law allows, our liability for any order is limited to the amount held in escrow for that order.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app before it applies.
